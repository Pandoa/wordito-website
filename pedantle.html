Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the software application Wordito for mobile devices. Wordito is a word puzzle game designed for both iOS and Android platforms.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Wordito does not collect personal information (such as name, email, or address) directly. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob for advertisements. To provide personalized and non-personalized ads, AdMob may access and use:

  • Advertising Identifiers: IDFA (iOS) and Google Advertising ID (Android).
  • Device Info: Model, OS version, and mobile carrier.
  • Location: Coarse/IP-based location data for regional ad relevance.

You may opt-out of personalized ads via your device system settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking; Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

3. In-App Purchases (IAP)

Wordito offers digital products for purchase. All transactions are handled by the respective app stores:

  • Apple App Store: Managed via your Apple ID and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store: Managed via your Google Account and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Wordito does not receive or store your credit card or billing information. We only receive a confirmation of purchase to unlock your features.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your location, we respect your right to privacy. Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, the "right to deletion" is exercised by clearing the app data on your device or opting out of ad tracking via your system settings.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, please contact us at:
support@wordito.com
